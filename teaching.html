Teaching

“Chemistry makes the most sense when you can see why things happen. I want my students to leave not with a list of rules, but with the intuition to work out the answers for themselves.”
— Renana Gershoni-Poranne

Courses at the Technion

First year

Principles of Chemistry A

The foundations of chemistry for first-year students: from atoms, light and quantum theory, through the periodic table and chemical bonding (from Lewis structures to molecular orbitals), to reactions, equilibrium, and acids and bases.

Lecturer since 2022
Advanced undergraduate

Molecular Orbitals in Organic Chemistry

A bridge between the quantum world and organic chemistry: qualitative MO theory, frontier orbitals, the Woodward–Hoffmann rules in ground and excited states, hyperconjugation and the anomeric effect. Students gain the intuition and tools to analyze – and predict – organic reactivity.

Lecturer since 2022
Advanced undergraduate

Structure and Activity in Organic Chemistry

The physical organic chemistry behind reaction mechanisms: potential energy surfaces, the Curtin–Hammett principle and the Hammond postulate, linear free-energy relationships, kinetics and isotope effects, and the reactive intermediates of organic chemistry – carbocations, carbanions and carbenes.

Lecturer since 2026
Hands-on lab · in English

Applied Computational Chemistry

A hands-on lab for students with little or no prior experience: running electronic-structure calculations with freely available software (ORCA, xTB), analyzing molecular properties and exploring reaction mechanisms, and finishing with an independent project written up as a research communication.

Lecturer since 2024

Before the Technion, Renana taught Organic Chemistry IV: Physical Organic Chemistry at ETH Zürich (2018–2021), and for seven years was a teaching assistant and senior teaching assistant at the Technion for general, organic and physical-methods courses (2008–2015).
