Join Us

We are always happy to hear from motivated people who are excited about aromaticity, computational chemistry and data-driven molecular design. The group believes in an inclusive and collaborative culture, where team-work and mutual respect are top priorities, and we work closely with collaborators around the world.

Fully-funded PhD and postdoctoral positions are available in:

  • Physical organic chemistry of polycyclic aromatic systems
  • Data-driven molecular design, including The COMPAS Project and machine-learning models
  • Mechanism elucidation, together with experimental groups

Read more on our Research page and see our recent publications.

Open positions

We are always looking for motivated students and coworkers to join our group. We have open positions for:

Undergraduate projects

MSc students

PhD students

Postdoctoral researchers

Postdocs who would like to apply for an independent fellowship to join the group are very welcome — we are happy to support your application.

How to apply

Please send the following to our group email:

  • A clear letter of motivation, explaining why you want to join our group and which of our projects (or maybe a new one you'd like to start!) you are particularly interested in
  • Your CV
